# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions.
Critique ideas and behavior without attacking people. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, doxxing, and publication of another person's private information
are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and limit participation to protect
contributors. Use GitHub's reporting controls for abusive content; do not repeat
private information in a public issue. Apply these expectations to everyone
participating in this project's public spaces.
